WHAT IT DOES

01

THE AGENT WATCHES

A small desktop agent notices new replays in your Siege folder and hands them off. You play; the analysis is waiting when you are done.

02

DEEP EXTRACTION

Beyond kills and deaths: damage events, drone clears, gadget volume and location events pulled out of the replay stream round by round.

03

LEADERBOARDS AND DRILLDOWNS

Rank by K/D, headshot rate per shot, drone clears or gadget volume. Compare two profiles head to head, or drill into a single map or operator.

02

WHAT IT DOESN'T DO

THE HONEST PART

It cannot analyse matches you have no replay for. Siege writes them locally, so the record has to exist on your machine first.

It is not a coach and does not tell you what to do next. It tells you what happened, precisely, and leaves the reading to you.

It is not live. Analysis happens after the match, from the recorded replay, not during the round.
